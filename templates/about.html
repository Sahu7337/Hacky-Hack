Sifter

AI-powered digital threat detection & digital trust platform

[ CREATED BY ]

Developed for Hackathon 2026 under Track 1: Cybersecurity & Digital Trust.

Track: Cybersecurity & Digital Trust
Mission: Empowering everyday internet users with instant, understandable digital threat intelligence to prevent phishing, fraudulent messages, and online scams.

[ TECHNOLOGY STACK & APIS ]

  • Google Gemini AI: Contextual threat reasoning, intent classification, and plain-English safety recommendations.
  • SerpAPI & Search Footprint Engine: Real-time domain verification, Google Search index presence check, and typosquatting detection.
  • Backend Architecture: Python 3, Flask, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and built-in neural NLP heuristics.
  • Frontend Design: Clean minimalist HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript adhering to a distraction-free black, white, and light grey palette.
