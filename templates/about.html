Created by AJ and the Team for Hackathon 2026 under Track 1: Cybersecurity & Digital Trust.

Track: Cybersecurity & Digital Trust
Mission: Empowering everyday internet users with instant, understandable digital threat intelligence to prevent phishing, fraudulent messages, and online scams.

Technology Stack & APIs

  • Google Gemini AI: Contextual threat reasoning, psychological intent classification, and plain-English safety recommendations.
  • SerpAPI & Search Footprint Engine: Real-time domain verification, Google Search index presence checks, and fraud report detection.
  • Backend Architecture: Python 3, Flask, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and built-in neural NLP heuristics.
  • Frontend Design: Clean minimalist HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript adhering to a distraction-free black, white, and slate grey palette.
